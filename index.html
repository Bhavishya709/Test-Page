Our Story

Real emotion, beautifully framed

Rooted in the heart of Hisar, Studio Parbhat has been at the forefront of visual storytelling for over two decades, setting benchmarks in luxury weddings, high-end maternity shoots and striking fashion portfolios. True art lives in authenticity, and our mission is to turn fleeting moments into memories you can cherish forever.

&

Founder Parbhat

The visionary behind the studio. Parbhat believes photography goes far beyond pointing a lens; it is about connecting with the soul of the subject. A stolen glance, a teardrop of happiness, a sudden burst of laughter: these unscripted emotions are what he captures. The best pictures are never posed, they are deeply felt.

The Expert Team

Our cinematographers and editors keep exploring new techniques and upgrading to the most advanced equipment, so every wedding film, fashion sequence or maternity portrait feels fresh, modern and vividly cinematic, with dynamic storytelling in every celebration.

Frequently asked

Where can I see your latest work?

Our Instagram, @studio_parbhat, is updated first with fresh weddings, pre-wedding shoots and reels. Full films are on our YouTube channel.

How do I check if my date is available?

Send us your date and city on WhatsApp or through the enquiry form below. We will get back to you personally.

Do you cover destination weddings?

Yes. We have photographed destination weddings and pre-wedding shoots beyond Hisar. Tell us your location and we will plan it with you.

Do you offer both photography and films?

Yes, our photographers and cinematographers work together, so you get stills and a cinematic film from a single studio.

Which events do you shoot?

Pre-wedding, engagement, cocktail, haldi, mehndi, wedding day, maternity, baby shoots, family portraits and fashion portfolios.
